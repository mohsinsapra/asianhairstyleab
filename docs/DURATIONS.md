# Durations to check

Every price comes from your printed price list. The **durations** are my proposals —
the flyer has none, and the booking calendar needs one per service to work out what is free.

Where SumUp already had a timing for the same treatment I kept it unchanged. Everything
else is a normal salon timing. Tell me any that are wrong and I will correct them; each is
one number in `js/services.js`.

A duration that is too short means customers get double-booked. Too long means you look
busier than you are. The long colour and treatment services matter most.
